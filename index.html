06Questions

Before you sign up.

Do I need a medical background?

No. Module one starts with the anatomy and vocabulary, and everything builds from there. Plenty of strong reps came from sales, sports or the military.

Is it only sports medicine?

The course covers orthopedics broadly: trauma, joints, fracture classification and the major companies' product lines. The surgery sim focuses on arthroscopic sports medicine cases, where many new reps start.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes. Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly, and you can cancel any time. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

What do I get with a mentor?

Pro + Mentor pairs you with a current or former orthopedic rep, vetted by us. You get two 30-minute calls a month, help with your résumé and interviews, and mock case coverage so you can practice a case with someone who has done the job.

How does Reps for Teams work?

Managers assign learning paths, add modules on their own products and track every rep's progress, quiz scores, sim results and readiness from one dashboard. Teams start at 5 seats, and one team can pilot it free for 30 days.

Is Reps affiliated with a device company?

No. Reps is independent. The product knowledge module teaches you about the major manufacturers so you can talk about any of them.
