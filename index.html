Questions

Good to know

Do I need a medical background?

No. The course starts with the anatomy and terminology you need and builds from there. Plenty of great reps came from sales, sports or the military.

Is this only for sports medicine?

The course covers orthopedics broadly: trauma, joints, fracture classification and company product lines. The surgery sim focuses on arthroscopic sports medicine cases, the most common place new reps start.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Reps is built phone first, so you can do a lesson in the parking lot before a case. It works on a laptop too.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes. Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly, and you can cancel any time. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

What do I get with a mentor?

Pro + Mentor pairs you with a current or former orthopedic rep, vetted by us. You get two 30-minute calls a month, help with your résumé and interviews, and mock case coverage so you can practice working a case with someone who has done the job.

How does Reps for Teams work?

Managers assign learning paths, add modules on their own products and track every rep's progress, quiz scores, sim results and readiness from one dashboard. Teams start at 5 seats, and one team can pilot it free for 30 days.

Is Reps affiliated with any device company?

No. Reps is independent. The product knowledge module teaches you about major manufacturers so you can talk confidently about any of them.
